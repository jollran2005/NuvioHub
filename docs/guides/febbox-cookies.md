# How to Get Your FebBox Cookie

Certain HTTP streaming addons require your personal FebBox ui cookie token to fetch direct high-speed streams.

::: warning Important Info
- **Do not log out of FebBox:** Closing the browser tab is fine, but clicking **Log Out** on febbox.com immediately invalidates your cookie session.
- **Privacy:** Never share your cookie string publicly. It grants temporary access to your FebBox account.
:::

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Go to [febbox.com](https://www.febbox.com) and log in (or create a free account).
2. Open your browser's developer tools — right-click the page → **Inspect** (or press F12).
3. Go to the **Application** tab (Chrome/Edge) or **Storage** tab (Firefox) → **Cookies** → `https://www.febbox.com`.
4. Find the cookie named `ui` and copy its **Value** — that long string is your token.
5. Paste it into the field above. Nothing else on that page is needed.
